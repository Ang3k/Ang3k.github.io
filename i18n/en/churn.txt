1 - Strategies and Procedures

@@ 7edf55d0e4
Just like cleaning a bedroom or a living room, cleaning a dataset requires a procedure: you can't start by sweeping and then throw the water on the floor! That is why, in this and other projects, I usually follow this order: removing useless columns, identifying and removing null values, identifying and removing outliers, creating dummy variables and encoding. This ensures a clean dataset, ready to work in an ML model.

@@ b54c6768a9

I then created two more functions: one that returned a dataframe with the sum of null values for each column, and another that generated a bar chart showing the percentage of missing values for each feature, that is, each column.
